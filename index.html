WHO WE ARE

A trusted partner in international dairy ingredients trading.

ROAZH is a French trading company based in Rennes, Brittany, specialized in international dairy ingredients trading.

Built on trust, commitment and proximity, ROAZH connects selected suppliers and buyers across international markets while maintaining a strong focus on responsiveness, reliability and long-term relationships.

OUR VISION

Building long-term relationships across international dairy markets.

International sourcing requires experience, trust and market understanding. ROAZH helps food industry partners access reliable sourcing opportunities through a global network of suppliers and market expertise.

Whether acting on behalf of buyers or suppliers, every operation is approached with the same commitment to transparency, responsiveness and long-term collaboration.
